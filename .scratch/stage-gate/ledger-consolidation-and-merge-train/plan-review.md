### What has to change before execution

Four things, in order of cost:

1. **Add `git add` to every conflict resolution** (A2, A7, A8). Without it the merge commit is refused and each step halts. `--theirs` is the correct side — that half of A2 is right.
2. **Give A8 the same `docs/` grep A7 has**, and add an explicit post-renumber step on both branches that re-resolves every `defer` row path in the review file. `merge_to_dev` fails hard on a stale path, so this is the difference between `--check-only` rc 0 and rc 1.
3. **Fix C1 and C2** so A0's stop condition does not fire on the plan's own bad data. The repo is on `dev`; `feat/reforge-catchup-leftovers` is 23 behind.
4. **Say what happens when `pnpm verify` goes red** in A5/A7/A8, particularly A8 where the red lands after two commits.

Beyond that, record the 376–380 reservation somewhere a session working from `dev` can see it (F8), and note in `merge-order.md` that commit-message ticket numbers will not be renumbered (F5). C6 is sound and the plan's central restructuring of the brief is justified — the merge order genuinely is free with respect to the fork pin.

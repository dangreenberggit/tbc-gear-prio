# Plan — phase-item-pool (revision 2)

## Goal

When this plan is done: (1) this repo carries a committed, re-runnable listing (`data/pool-listings/ret-p3.md`, `feral-p3.md`) that states, per item, why it is in or out of the phase-3 candidate pool, cross-checked item-by-item against the wowsims fork's own item database, with every wowsims-only item carrying a mechanical reason or an explicit "unexplained — owner question" flag, gated by a new `pool-listings:check` step in `pnpm verify`; (2) the fork's bundled universe copies byte-match `data/universes/` again and a new `fork-universes:check` gate fails when they drift (ticket 211 closes); (3) the Upgrades tab names the phase in plain words — the prune checkbox, BiS view filter, and assumptions drawer say "Phase 3", not "this phase" — and carries the page's own phase selector, bound to the same `sim` state so tab and page cannot disagree; (4) a post-sim raid filter narrows a completed shopping list by zone without re-simming, and zoneless gear (badge, crafted, PvP, rep, world) stays reachable through labelled buckets rather than vanishing. Nothing about ranking math changes; no pool membership changes beyond refreshing the fork's stale copies.

## Approach

**Membership stays local; wowsims becomes the auditor and the vocabulary.** The pool the tab sims keeps coming from this repo's assembled universes (bundled in the fork, as today). The wowsims item DB — which the owner asked to use "ideally" — becomes the primary for *phase* values, *zone naming*, and *completeness auditing*: a new listing script builds the wowsims-primary candidate membership (defined below), diffs it against the universe, and classifies every difference by a rule it can cite (weapon/hand exclusion, `eligible_d7` stat screen, stub-only sim effect, class allowlist, no recognized source route). What it cannot classify it lists as an owner question, never silently adds.

The strongest rejected alternative is making the wowsims DB primary for membership (the owner's stated ideal, Q1 candidate (a)). It loses on measured data, not preference: the fork DB carries only `drop`/`crafted`/`rep` source kinds (reviewer census: 2821/1113/111) and records **no `sources` entry at all** for badge-vendor, PvP, and tier-token items (measured on Bloodlust Brooch 29383, the Vengeful Gladiator's weapons, and every Lightbringer/Thunderheart piece — C10; the reviewer independently hunted `tools/database/`, `assets/db_inputs/atlasloot_db.json`, and the `DifficultyVendor` enum for a wowsims-side carrier of those origins and found none), so a wowsims-primary pool could not say *where an item comes from*, which is half the goal; its phase-≤3 spec-eligible Epic membership is several times the curated universe and mostly phase-1 leftovers with no curation and no EP/bisTags (C12); and its phase→zone knowledge lives in Go tooling (`InferPhase`), not in anything the TS page can read (C9). The local universe already *is* a wowsims-derived pool — `vendor/wowsims/db.json` is its primary input (C5) — so "wowsims ideally, local as backup" is honored at the data level: wowsims values win wherever wowsims has them (phase: zero disagreements measured on both p3 universes, C11; zone names: universe zone strings are canonical db.json `zones[].name` strings, C13), and the local assembly supplies only what wowsims lacks (sources for source-less items, curation, bisTags, EP).

### Open questions Q1–Q4

**Q1 — pool source on the site: candidate (c), hybrid with explicit per-field precedence, wins.**
Precedence rule: *membership and source metadata* — local universe; *phase* — wowsims DB (audited: the listing fails loudly on any disagreement; today there are zero, C11); *zone vocabulary* — wowsims `zones[].name` (already true by construction, C13); *completeness* — wowsims DB is the reference the universe is audited against.
The audit's **wowsims-primary membership definition**, stated in full because explainable membership is the point (F6): items with `phase <= maxPhase`, `quality == Epic (4)`, spec-eligible per the assembler's own `SPEC_PROFILES` (class allowlist, armor type, weapon/hand rules, relic type). **Legendary (`quality == 5`) is deliberately excluded**: the fork DB holds 18 legendaries, two of them phase 3 (the Warglaives, 32837/32838 — reviewer-verified), and none is ret/feral-usable under the same spec rules (1H sword/dagger; ret excludes 1H weapons, feral cannot use swords or daggers); the engine additionally strips the Kael'thas temporary legendaries (`isKaelTempLegendary`, `upgrades_tab.tsx:405`). The listing states both rules in its own header. Rare-and-below is excluded from the *audit* because the measured Rare phase-3 gap is zero for both specs (the one C12 number the reviewer reproduced).
- Dropped (a) wowsims-primary membership: no sources on badge/PvP/token items (C10), an uncurated membership several times the universe size (C12), no TS-side phase→zone table (C9).
- Dropped (b) local-only with wowsims as commentary: leaves the owner's stated risk — a missing source — with no gate; the measured reverse gap is real (S3 ret PvP armor and the BT quest necks are absent, C15/C16a), and only the wowsims cross-check finds the next one.
- Win condition (pre-stated, property-based per F1): for ret-p3 and feral-p3, the committed listing prints both memberships and their symmetric difference; every universe-only item carries its source; every wowsims-only item carries a reason category or an "unexplained" flag; **zero** phase disagreements; **zero** phase-3 raid drops in the "unexplained" bucket. The listing establishes the counts; the plan does not predict them.
- Measurement: `pnpm pool-listings:check` (regenerates and byte-compares) plus reading the two committed listings.

**Q2 — phase naming and selection: candidate (a) plus shared-state selector, wins.**
The tab's strings name the phase from `sim.getPhase()` rendered through the existing `common.phases.N` strings ("Phase 3 (2.2 - T6)", C20), and the tab mounts the existing `makePhaseSelector` (`ui/core/components/inputs/other_inputs.ts:77-88`) bound to the *same* `sim` — so it is the page's setting, surfaced, not an override. The tab's `markStale` handler (subscribed to `gearChangeEmitter`, `talentsChangeEmitter`, and `sim.changeEmitter`, C21) already refreshes the placeholder on phase change, so the phase-named labels refresh in the same handler with no new subscriptions.
- Dropped (b) tab-local phase override: a second phase state can silently disagree with the page's — exactly what the win condition forbids; and the Gear tab's selector modal already mounts the same picker (`gear_picker/item_list.tsx:218`), so the shared-state control is the established pattern.
- Win: no rendered string says "this phase"; prune checkbox, BiS filter, and drawer name the phase; changing the phase anywhere updates the tab.
- Measurement: rendered strings on a served page at phase 2 and phase 3 (Step 9).

**Q3 — zone/content filters: candidate (a) post-sim raid filter, wins, with the F4 ruling built in; boss filter deferred.**
The filter's vocabulary is `zoneKeyOf` — the same key `groupBy: 'raid'` already uses (`engine/view.ts:75-80`): a zone name for `raid`/`token` sources, and a labelled bucket from the existing `ZONELESS_SOURCE_LABELS` ("Badge vendor", "Crafted", "Reputation vendor", "PvP vendor", "World drop", "Source not recorded") for everything else. The raid `<select>` offers "All" plus every bucket present in the pool, so **zoneless items do not vanish: they are first-class filter targets under their own labelled buckets** — chosen over "always visible under any raid selection" because a player narrowing to Black Temple wants a Black Temple list, and the bucket options keep badge/crafted gear one click away in the same control instead of mixing it into every raid's list; it also keeps the filter and `groupBy: 'raid'` speaking one vocabulary rather than two (the brief's no-third-model rule). This requires extending `applyView`'s raid matching from zone-only (`matchesZone`, `view.ts:58-60`) to `zoneKeyOf`-based — `view.ts` is a ported engine file, so the change lands in `packages/core/src/view.ts` first with a unit test (pure function, direct unit tests allowed per AGENTS.md Testing), then ports to the fork with a PROVENANCE re-hash, the mechanism the last stage already exercised (`e79916172`). A confirmed consequence, accepted under the bucket ruling: `renderSubTabs()` derives the slot-tab set from the same filtered view, so a narrow filter also narrows which slot tabs appear — every item stays reachable via its own bucket, and Step 9 measures the slot-tab set rather than leaving it implicit.
- Dropped (b) pre-sim zone pruning: it changes which candidates are simmed, so narrowing costs a re-run — the win condition says "without re-simming".
- Deferred: the boss sub-filter. Reason to record for the owner: `applyView` supports it, but ticket 35 (multi-zone items bucket arbitrarily) makes boss grouping misleading today, and the raid filter alone answers "content I will actually run". One ticket notes the deferral.
- Win: on a completed run, selecting a raid or a zoneless bucket narrows the list to items with a matching source, instantly, reversibly; no item is unreachable under every filter value.
- Measurement: rendered list on a completed run under each filter value, including the zoneless-item counts and the slot-tab set (Step 9).

**Q4 — bundled vs runtime, and honesty gates.** Bundled: the universes and EP weights in the fork (static imports, unchanged — the page must work offline from its own dist, and the tab already works this way). Runtime: nothing new is fetched. Two gates keep the bundles honest: `fork-universes:check` (new; byte-compares all eight copied files against their `data/` sources; skips cleanly with a message when the gitignored clone is absent, same pattern as `check_engine_port_drift.py:87`) and `pool-listings:check` (new; regenerates the listings from `vendor/wowsims/db.json` + universes and byte-compares; CI-safe because `sync:wowsims:restore` restores `db.json` before `pnpm verify`, C7; fails loudly, not skips, when `db.json` is absent — the precedent is `check_rep_tables.py`, which already reads that file and names the restore command). One-command refresh: `python scripts/sync_fork_universes.py --write`.
- Win: `pnpm verify` fails when either bundled copy drifts; measured today it *would* fail — **five** of the eight copied files have drifted (C14) — and passes after Step 2.

### What was checked and what could not be established (per brief question)

**Local:** all sources enumerated in C4–C6; exclusions enumerated in C6 (heroic allowlist, weapon-type excludes, `eligible_d7`, stub-only effects, `excludedNoSource`); known-gap tickets read (253 closed; 259, 173, 58, 17, 56, 89, 172, 211, 35, 59 open). **wowsims:** per-item fields, filter machinery, phase provenance established (C8–C10); *not established*: whether `InferPhase`'s "crafted ilvl 128–141 → phase 3" is right for items that actually shipped in patch 2.3 (Swiftsteel/Swiftstrike Shoulders carry phase 3 in the DB but are 2.3 recipes — SME question, Step 5 ticket). **Join:** established as the precedence rule above; *not established from data*: whether the gap items in C16a/C16b *should* join the pools — Step 5 puts them to the owner instead of deciding. Also noted for the owner: upstream `CURRENT_PHASE` is still Phase 2 (C19), so a fresh page pools for phase 2 until the player moves the selector; making the tab default differently would diverge from wowsims' own statement and is not done here. The Q1 inversion of the owner's stated ideal goes to the owner as an explicit confirmation item at hand-off (Gate B ruling).

# Crafted gear whose pattern drops in a raid is not in the list

A player at phase 5 never sees crafted gear such as Gloves of Immortal
Dusk (34370), whose pattern drops in Sunwell Plateau. It is absent from
`data/universes/feral-p5.json`, one of the five items ticket 500 lists.

## The owner's words, 2026-10-11

"It's kind of yikes that this was missed, and even more yikes that it
needs to be added manually. There should be a programmatic way to check and
add such items (for example if we add more specs)"

## Why it is missing

- The wowsims item database gives a crafted item a `crafted` source with a
  profession and a spell id, and no zone. The list builder admits an item
  by zone, heroic dungeon or reputation (`scripts/assemble_universe.py`
  `source_zones`, `:1862-1870`, reads a zone only from `raid` and `token`
  sources), so a crafted item has no route in.
- The builder already knows which raid a pattern drops in:
  `data/two-hop/raid-recipes.json` (generated by `scripts/parse_atlasloot.py`,
  146 entries) maps product to recipe zone, and the builder copies it onto
  the source as `recipeZone` (`:2381-2385`). That field is only a note; no
  membership check reads it.
- `:2633` is the db-phase route's skip for items that already have a
  source, so the nine db-phase specs do not admit these items by phase
  either.

## What was measured (2026-10-11)

A throwaway prototype (`completeness.py` in a session scratchpad, may be
gone) found 82 unique crafted items, own phase 2 or later, that a spec can
wear and that are absent from that spec's phase-5 list, across all 11 specs
(18 to 79 per spec). Joining those 82 ids to `raid-recipes.json`: 65 have a
raid-zone pattern (Sunwell Plateau 33, Black Temple 16, Serpentshrine
Cavern 16); 17 have no entry there (for example the phase-2 blacksmithing
weapons such as Blazefury 28427, and engineering goggles such as
Justicebringer 2000 Specs 32472). Those 17 are not this ticket's route;
ticket 610's gate must give them a reason or a route.

## What would close this

1. A membership route in `scripts/assemble_universe.py`: a crafted item
   whose pattern drops in a raid of the list's phase (from
   `raid-recipes.json`) is in that spec's list, for all 11 specs.
2. Each changed universe's diff is listed in the closing note (count of
   added items per spec and phase), and 34370 is in
   `data/universes/feral-p5.json` and its fork copy.
3. `pnpm verify` rc=0.

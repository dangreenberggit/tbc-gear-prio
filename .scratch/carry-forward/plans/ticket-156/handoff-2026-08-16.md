## 5. What "Candidates" means

Misunderstood repeatedly this session; stated here to stop the next repeat.

The box is **not** "how many items get tested" — every eligible item is
screened regardless. It caps how many get the **full-accuracy** sim, applied
**after** screening and promotion (`rank.ts:1206-1213`).

1 baseline sim → screen **all** P pool items at `DEFAULT_SCREEN_ITERATIONS =
1000` (`promotion.ts:19`; rings/trinkets cost 2 screens each,
`pool.ts:340-351`) → promote on four OR'd criteria (global top-150 by screening
delta, best-in-slot, set-package member, owned — `promotion.ts:82-138`) →
**cap applies here** → full sims at the tab's Iterations → set-bonus packages →
paired replication of the top 8 under 4 extra seeds.

- **Owned rows are exempt from the cap and additional to it**
  (`rank.ts:1212`). At Candidates 20, the earlier session's `38/71` progress
  counter works out to **34** full-sim candidates: 20 capped + 14 owned.
- **Empty = no cap** (`upgrades_tab.tsx:321-326` → `rank.ts:1210`): the
  slowest, most thorough run. The screening floor (~P sims at 1000 iterations)
  is unavoidable from the UI.
- The tab never sets `screenIterations`, `fullPool`, `promoteTopK` or
  `promoteTopJ`, so **racing is always on in the browser** and screening is
  always 1000 iterations.

## 8. Other defects found — filed separately, do not fix inside 156

1. **`candidateCap` applies in EP order, not screening-delta order**
   (`rank.ts:1206-1213`), while the comment directly above claims the opposite.
   Changes **which** items a user sees; **does not change how many sims run**

   `simCandidates.length`), so it does not invalidate a timing. Second-order
   caveat: a different item set can change the set-bonus package sim count.

3. **The assumptions drawer is factually wrong** (`upgrades_tab.tsx:738-741`):
   tells users racing "was not shipped" and that a cap is "an EP-order
   preselection, not a second, cheaper ranking pass". Racing is shipped and on
   by default.

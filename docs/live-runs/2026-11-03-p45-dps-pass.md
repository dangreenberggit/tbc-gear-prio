# Phase 4 and phase 5 live pass over the 11 DPS specs (2026-11-03)

## Verdict

All 22 runs (11 DPS specs, phases 4 and 5) finished and count: each JSON has
`done: true`, no panic, no timeout, and the harness exited 0. The SME seats
rated 6 runs `trust` and 16 `trust-with-caveats`; none `do-not-trust`.

wowsims ships its own next-phase preset for 13 of the 22 runs (balance,
elemental, warlock, feralcat, enhancement and warrior at both phases;
hunter at phase 4). Most items those presets add show as gains. The
exceptions, found by diffing each start preset slot by slot against the
next preset under `vendor/tbc-new-fork/ui/specs/*/gear_sets/` and looking
up each new item's name in the run's `rows`:

- **Losses where one swap replaces a piece of the worn 4-piece T6 set**
  (ticket 523), all at phase 5: balance head, shoulders and hands (-60.4 to
  -68); elemental head, chest and hands (-35.6 to -55.8); warlock head,
  hands and legs (-93.3 to -108); feralcat chest, hands and legs (-3.7 to
  -17.3). The set pieces are the start preset's items with a db.json
  `setName`.
- **Other losses:** feralcat p5 Cursed Vision of Sargeras -60.8 (replaces
  Wolfshead Helm), Thunderheart Waistguard -24.9 (replaces Belt of
  One-Hundred Deaths) and Clutch of Demise -10.1 (replaces Choker of
  Endless Nightmares); enhancement p5 Hand of the
  Deceiver -18.3, Mounting Vengeance -33.1 and Skyshatter Pauldrons -9.9;
  warlock p5 Loop of Forged Power -4.5; warlock p4 Carved Witch Doctor's
  Stick -2; hunter p4 Shadowmoon Destroyer's Drape -0.5.
- **No row:** elemental p4's second Ring of Ancient Knowledge (ticket 309);
  Sunflare and Heart of the Pit at phase 5 for balance, elemental and
  warlock, because the tab holds one-handers out while a two-hander is worn
  (closed ticket 700).

The pass filed four new tickets (750-753), added evidence to four open
tickets (309, 523, 620, 641), and found nothing new for 746. For epic 452
this pass is evidence about the tab's output per spec and phase; it meets
none of the epic's four "done" bullets in full (§ For epic 452), and one
question goes to the owner (Q-452-golden).

  (281907), The Horseman's Signet Ring (281911) and Witches Band (281915)
  appear in all 22 runs. The Horseman's Helm (281919, plate) appears only
  for ret and warrior. The Horseman's Blade (281923) appears in no run.

  the cutoff (-72.5, -73.9), costing about the worn totem's value since its
  effect is an attack-power proc. Enhancement shows it at phase 4 at +7.8,
  above the cutoff; at phase 5 the start preset already wears it, so it has

  `119b9f676` and `13a980cf3`, so no before/after pair exists, and the
  phase-4 feral run matches the 10-25 run row for row.
